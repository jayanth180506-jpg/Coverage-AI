# Weak Coverage Regions

Threshold: predicted/measured RSSI below -70 dBm. Summaries use held-out spatial test predictions, grouped into 5x5 bins per building. Binning is a coarse report, not a router-placement recommendation.

- Regions summarized: 25
- Regions with mean predicted RSSI below threshold: 25
- Detailed measured and predicted weak fractions: `models/comparison/weak_coverage_regions.csv`
